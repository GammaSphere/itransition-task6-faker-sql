Geolocation: uniform on the sphere

The map uses the Behrmann projection, which preserves area, so a uniform distribution on the globe looks uniform here: the poles are not crowded. About 71% of the dots land at sea, like 71% of the Earth’s surface.

Users per 10° band of latitude

Latitude is drawn as asin(2u − 1), so its density follows cos(latitude): bands near the equator hold far more surface than bands near the poles. Drawing latitude uniformly in degrees would give every band the same count.
